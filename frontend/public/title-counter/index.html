Why Etsy titles are limited to 140 characters

Etsy caps listing titles at 140 characters. Anything longer gets cut off in search results. More importantly, the first 30–40 characters carry the most ranking weight, so your most important keywords should go up front.

Frequently asked questions

How long can an Etsy title be?

140 characters maximum. This counter shows you exactly where you stand.

Do spaces count in an Etsy title?

Yes — spaces and punctuation all count toward the 140-character limit.

What part of the title matters most?

The first 30–40 characters. Put your strongest keywords there, and keep the rest readable for humans.
